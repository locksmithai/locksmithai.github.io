How it picks

Every ranking on this page is from 2026 games only, in EPA per play (1 = best of 32), and injuries come from the latest official report. Each game gets a predicted margin and total from team efficiency (EPA per play on offense and defense), quarterback ratings, Elo, injuries weighted by playing time, rest, home field and weather. The model starts from the market line and only moves off it where those stats have historically disagreed with it. A Lock is a 3+ point gap from the line, a Lean is 1.5 to 3, and anything smaller is a Pass. The key beside each pick cuts deeper as the gap grows.
